My Story

Financial competence takes trust — let me earn yours.

Before you decide we're the right match for some of the most vulnerable parts of your life, let me introduce myself with a few quick facts and the "why" behind the work I do.

My name is George Curbelo and I'm 46 years old. I've been married for 20 years and together my wife and I have two daughters, ages 20 and 15. Originally from the Bronx, I spent about 15 years in PA before moving to Palm Harbor, Florida in 2018 — and I haven't missed the snow once.

I've poured my coaching and mentoring skills into a clear focus: helping people achieve their financial goals. I went through my own debt-free journey a couple of years ago, so I know the heartache and despair — and the highs of success and hope.

The Turning Point

August 2019: my moment of clarity

I was living paycheck to paycheck. No budget. No control. No purpose for the money flowing in and out. No real plan for financial independence.

I wanted more for my family. I knew a better financial foundation was the key step in the right direction — and once I figured out my "why," everything changed.

Now my job is helping you find your why — and giving you the roadmap to live by it.
