Back To School Budgeting - Episode 523

By George Curbelo August 25, 2026 3 min read
Listen to this episode of the Crushing Debt Podcast:

Back to school season is one of the biggest spending events of the year — and most families get caught off guard every single time. From school supplies and new clothes to technology, sports fees, and activity costs, the bills add up fast. In this episode, Shawn and George break down how to get ahead of back-to-school spending so it doesn't derail the progress you've made all summer.

Whether you're shopping for kindergarteners or college students, the principles are the same: plan ahead, set a number, and stick to it. Tune in for practical, no-nonsense strategies to make this back-to-school season your most financially confident one yet.

Key Topics Discussed:

  • The Real Cost of Back to School — How much families actually spend and why it sneaks up on even the most budget-conscious parents
  • Building a Back-to-School Budget — How to set a realistic number before you ever walk into a store
  • Smart Shopping Strategies — Sales timing, list discipline, and the difference between needs and wants when school supply shopping
  • College Back-to-School Costs — Dorm setup, meal plans, technology, and the expenses no one warns you about
  • Recovering If You've Already Overspent — Practical steps to reset your budget and stay on track for the rest of the year
