Crushing Debt Guide to Holiday Giving — Episode 442

By George Curbelo Dec 10, 2024 3 min read
Listen to this episode of the Crushing Debt Podcast:

Welcome to December! We hope everyone had a great Thanksgiving holiday and were able to reflect on what you are thankful for this year.

Now we focus on the holidays, which means buying gifts. How do you control your holiday spending? Do you control your holiday spending?

In this week's episode Shawn & George talk about different ideas to keep holiday spending under control, including:

  • Shopping on Black Friday, Cyber Monday, etc.
  • Setting a budget
  • Taking advantage of holiday deals
  • Shopping early (before Thanksgiving)
  • Using all those reward points you've been saving throughout the year
  • Avoiding shipping (order online and pick up in the store)
  • Buying wrapping paper and other supplies at discount stores
  • DIY presents
  • Setting expectations on kids' gifts
  • Setting spending limits
