Why I Won't Panic When an Emergency Happens

By George Curbelo May 25, 2023 2 min read

I meet a lot of people who're looking for help with their finances and money habits. And when I interact with them, I realize that most folks don't really understand emergency funds as well as they could.

This lack of understanding often impairs their ability to build wealth and peace in their finances.

The role and responsibilities of an emergency fund are very different from those of a savings or checking account.

The emergency fund topic answers:

  • What an emergency fund is
  • How much you should have
  • How to build it

All so you can worry less, achieve more, and fulfill your financial and life goals!

If you want to learn more about emergency funds and whether one will work for you, check out this comprehensive guide to emergency funds by The Accountable Network: 'Why I Won't Panic When an Emergency Happens'.
