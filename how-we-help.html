The Process

A simple, structured path forward

Coaching isn't magic — it's a repeatable process of clarity, plan, action, and accountability.

Free Assessment

A 30-minute call to talk through where you are, what's stuck, and what you want. No pressure, no obligation — and you'll walk away with at least one immediate idea you can try.

Build the Plan

We dig into your numbers, goals, and the deeper "why" behind them. By the end of our first working session you'll have a budget you understand and a 90-day roadmap.

Honest Answers

Common questions, before we get on a call

Is this just for people drowning in debt?

Not at all. About half of our clients are doing "fine" on paper but feel like their money has no direction. We coach people across the spectrum — from heavy debt to high earners building wealth.

How is a coach different from a financial advisor?

Financial advisors typically manage your investments. A coach helps you build the habits, budget, and mindset that make investing possible in the first place. Most clients eventually need both — and we partner with vetted advisors when you're ready.

Do you coach individuals or only couples?

Both. Many of our most powerful sessions are with couples (because alignment changes everything), but plenty of individuals work with us solo, including young professionals, single parents, and retirees.

Is everything virtual?

Yes — sessions happen over Zoom or phone, with email and text in between. That means you can coach from anywhere in the country, and we can work around your real schedule.

What if I can't afford it right now?

Have the conversation anyway. We have multiple program tiers and we'd rather find a fit than turn you away. The free assessment is genuinely free, no card required.
